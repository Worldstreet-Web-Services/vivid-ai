# Recipe: school (a school, academy or training centre: public site plus a parent and student portal)

Pages: Home, About, Academics or Programmes, Admissions (apply), News and Events, Gallery,
Contact; Portal (signed in): student results, timetable, fees, notices. Owner area at
/admin: applications, students, classes, results entry, fees.

Home: hero with students in uniform (generated, Black African, joyful), the school's line,
Apply and Visit; quick facts row (founded, students, ratio); programmes as cards; a
principal's welcome; upcoming events and latest news; gallery; footer with address, phone,
term dates.

Admissions: requirements, fees, a stepped application form (child, parent, previous
school, documents), status page; the office approves from /admin.

Portal: parent or student sign-in; results by term as a table with grades and remarks,
downloadable; timetable; fees with balance and Pay (Paystack when the spec has it);
notices.

Data: programmes, classes, students, parents, applications, terms, results, fees and
payments, events, posts.

Minimums for a first build: the full public site with 6 programmes and 8 news or events,
the application form saving, the portal with seeded results and fees for demo accounts,
admin at /admin with applications and results entry.
