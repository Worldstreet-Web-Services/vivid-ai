# Recipe: portfolio (photographer, designer, creator)

Pages: Work (home), Project, About, Contact.

Work: minimal header (name left, three links right), a masonry-like grid (columns-1
sm:columns-2 lg:columns-3 with break-inside-avoid) of project covers, each with a title and
year under it. No hero; the work is the hero.

Project: a large cover, a two-column intro (role, year, client, tools on the left; a
paragraph on the right), then images stacked with generous space (space-y-8).

About: portrait (aspect-[3/4]), a few paragraphs, a client list. Contact: email, socials,
a short form.

Typography carries this recipe: a distinctive heading font, lots of whitespace, muted
metadata. Dark mode optional but consistent.
