# Recipe: wedding (a wedding or celebration site: story, schedule, RSVP, registry, gallery)

Pages: Home, Our story, Schedule, Travel and Stay, RSVP, Registry or Gifts, Gallery, FAQ.
Owner area at /admin: RSVPs, guest list, gifts.

Home: the couple's names in a display serif with a date and place, a countdown, a hero
portrait (generated: a Black African couple, warm editorial light), RSVP button, the
schedule preview, footer with hashtag and contact for questions.

Schedule: events (traditional, church, reception) with times, dress code, venue and
directions. Travel: hotels with rates, transport, what to know. RSVP: find your invite by
name or code, accept or decline per guest, meal choice, song request, message; confirmation
and the ability to edit later. Registry: cash gift with Paystack or bank details, a list of
gifts with reserve; Gallery: engagement photos and, after the day, the wedding album.

Data: couple and events, guests and invites (names, code, party size), RSVPs, gifts,
photos, FAQs.

Minimums for a first build: every page with the spec's details, RSVP by code working and
saving, registry with the payment or bank details, 12 gallery photos, admin at /admin
with the RSVP list and export.
