# Recipe: travel (tours, trips, experiences or a travel agency with itineraries)

Pages: Home, Trips or Tours, Trip, Book, About, Reviews, Contact. Owner area at /admin:
bookings, departures, trip editor.

Home: hero with a destination photo and the promise, a search or quick filters (where,
when, budget), featured trips as cards (photo, name, days, next departure, price from,
rating), why travel with us, how it works, reviews with photos, a Instagram-like grid,
footer with licence and contact.

Trip: gallery, overview strip (days, group size, difficulty, meals), day-by-day itinerary
as an accordion, what is included and not, departures with seats left and price, a Book
panel, FAQ, reviews. Book: departure, travellers, details, deposit or full payment
(Paystack), confirmation with what to bring.

Data: trips (name, slug, destination, days, description, itinerary, includes, excludes,
photos, price), departures (trip, date, seats, price), bookings, reviews.

Minimums for a first build: 8 trips with itineraries and 3 photos each, departures with
seat counts, booking to confirmation, reviews, admin at /admin with bookings and
departures.
