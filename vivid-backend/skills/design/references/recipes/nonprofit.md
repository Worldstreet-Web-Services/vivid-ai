# Recipe: nonprofit (a charity, NGO, church, foundation or campaign with donations)

Pages: Home, About or Our work, Programmes or Ministries, Donate, Events, Get involved
(volunteer), Contact, Blog or News. Owner area at /admin: donations, volunteers, events.

Home: hero with a portrait of real-feeling people (generated, Black African, warm light),
the mission in one sentence, Donate and Volunteer buttons; impact numbers row (counts up);
programmes as cards; a story or testimonial; upcoming events; a Donate band with preset
amounts; partners; footer with registration number and address.

Donate: preset amounts (₦2,000, ₦5,000, ₦10,000, other), one-off or monthly toggle,
dedication note, donor details, Paystack; thank-you page with a receipt number and share
buttons; donors never need an account.

Programmes: each with photos, what it does, who it helps, how to help. Get involved:
volunteer form (skills, availability), saved and thanked. Events: list and detail with
RSVP.

Data: programmes, donations (amount, frequency, donor, reference, status), volunteers,
events and RSVPs, posts.

Minimums for a first build: the full home page with real impact numbers from the spec, 4
programmes with photos, the donation flow to the thank-you page, volunteer form saving, 3
events, admin at /admin with donations and volunteers.
