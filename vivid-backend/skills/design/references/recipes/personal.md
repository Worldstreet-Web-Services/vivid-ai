# Recipe: personal (a personal site, resume, link-in-bio, creator or public figure page)

Pages: Home (everything on one page for most; separate About, Work, Writing, Contact
when there is enough), optional Links page (the link-in-bio: a phone-width column of
big buttons).

Home: a large portrait or a strong wordmark, name and one line of who you are and what
you do, a short bio in plain words, current role and location, what you are open to,
selected work or highlights as cards, writing or talks list, a contact row (email, socials
as icon buttons), footer with a last-updated line.

Resume: a clean printable page (experience with dates, education, skills as pills,
languages), Download PDF via print styles, the same content as the site data.

Links page: avatar, name, one line, a stack of full-width pill buttons with icons, socials
row; theme from the palette; works as the whole site when the spec is only that.

Data: profile, experience, projects, links, posts.

Minimums for a first build: the home page complete with real details from the spec, a
printable resume page when asked, the links page when asked, no lorem ipsum anywhere.
