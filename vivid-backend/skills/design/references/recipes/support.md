# Recipe: support (a help centre with articles, a ticket desk and live chat for a business)

Pages: Help home (search), Category, Article, Contact or Submit a ticket, My tickets
(signed in), Status page (optional). Agent area at /admin: inbox, ticket, macros,
articles editor.

Help home: a big search, categories as tiles with article counts, popular articles, a
"still stuck? contact us" band with a ticket form and hours; Article: content with steps
and screenshots, "was this helpful", related articles.

Tickets: form (subject, category, description, attachments, contact), a ticket page with
the conversation, status badge and replies; email or WhatsApp notification note. Agent
inbox: filters (open, pending, mine, unassigned), a ticket view with the conversation,
customer context, macros, assign, status, internal notes; a chat widget when the spec
asks for live chat.

Data: categories, articles, tickets (subject, category, status, priority, requester,
assignee), messages, macros, agents.

Minimums for a first build: 6 categories with 24 articles, working search, ticket submit
to a ticket page, the agent inbox at /admin with assignment and replies, seeded tickets in
every status.
