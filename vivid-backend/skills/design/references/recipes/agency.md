# Recipe: agency (a studio, consultancy, law firm, architects, or any services firm)

Pages: Home, Services, Work or Case studies, Case study, About and Team, Insights (optional),
Contact (with a project enquiry form). Owner area at /admin: enquiries only.

Home: a confident hero (a big statement headline, one line of what you do for whom, one
button), a client logos strip, services as a numbered list with short descriptions,
selected work as large cards with the outcome as the title, a process in four steps,
testimonials with names and roles, a CTA band, footer with offices.

Case study: cover, client and sector, the challenge, what we did, the result with numbers,
a gallery, a quote, next case. Services: each with what you get, who it is for, how we
work, starting price when the spec has one. Team: portraits (generated, Black African
professionals) with roles.

Contact: an enquiry form (name, company, budget range, timeline, what you need) that
saves and thanks; office address, phone, WhatsApp, map when a key exists.

Data: services, case studies (title, client, sector, challenge, approach, results,
images), team, testimonials, enquiries, posts.

Minimums for a first build: 6 services, 6 case studies with images and numbers, 6 team
members with portraits, 4 testimonials, the enquiry form saving, admin at /admin with
enquiries.
