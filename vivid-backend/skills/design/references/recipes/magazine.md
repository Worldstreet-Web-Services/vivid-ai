# Recipe: magazine (a blog, news site, publication or content brand)

Pages: Home, Category, Article, Author, Search, About, Newsletter. Owner area at /admin:
posts editor with drafts, categories, subscribers.

Home: a lead story with a large image and a headline in the display face, a row of three
secondary stories, then category sections each with one big and three small cards, a
newsletter band, a "most read" rail, footer with sections and socials.

Article: category eyebrow, a headline up to two lines, standfirst, author card with date and
reading time, a full-width image with caption, body with pull quotes and inline images at
max-w-prose, share row, tags, related stories, comments only if the spec asks.

Category: list with pagination; Author: bio and their stories; Search: instant results.

Data: posts (title, slug, standfirst, body as markdown or blocks, cover, category, author,
tags, published_at, status), categories, authors, subscribers.

Minimums for a first build: 16 posts across 5 categories with cover images (generated,
editorial), 3 authors with portraits, the article page fully styled for long text, working
search and category pages, newsletter sign-up saving, admin at /admin with the editor.
