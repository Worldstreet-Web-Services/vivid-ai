# Recipe: course (online courses, a school of one, or a learning platform)

Pages: Home, Courses, Course (curriculum and enrol), Lesson player (signed in), My
learning, Instructor or About, Pricing. Owner area at /admin: courses and lessons editor,
students, sales.

Home: hero with the outcome ("Learn to ..." in plain words), a preview video or the
instructor's portrait, Enrol button, social proof numbers (students, hours, rating);
featured courses as cards (thumbnail, title, level, lessons count, price, rating); how it
works in three steps; instructor story; testimonials with photos; FAQ; footer.

Course: hero with the title, what you will learn as a checklist, curriculum as an accordion
of modules and lessons with durations and free previews marked, the price card sticky on
desktop (price, Enrol, includes list, money-back line), instructor card, reviews.

Lesson player: a left curriculum rail, the video or reading in the centre, notes and
resources below, Mark complete and Next; progress saved; certificate page when complete.

Data: courses (title, slug, level, price, thumbnail, description, outcomes), modules,
lessons (title, type, duration, video or content, free), enrolments, progress, reviews.

Minimums for a first build: 4 courses with 3 modules and 12 lessons each, a working
player with progress, enrolment to the player (payment when the spec has it), admin at
/admin with the course editor and students.
