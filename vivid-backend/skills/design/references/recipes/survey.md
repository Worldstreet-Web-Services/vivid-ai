# Recipe: survey (forms, quizzes, polls, applications, registrations, assessments)

Pages: Home or a form's own landing, the Form (one question per screen or a single page),
Thank you, Results (for quizzes: the score and explanations). Owner area at /admin: forms
builder, responses table and charts, export CSV.

Form: a progress bar, one question per screen on phones with Next and Back, big tap
targets, question types (short text, long text, choice, multiple, scale, rating, date,
file upload, yes or no), validation inline, conditional questions when the spec asks,
autosave of partial answers in the browser, a review step, submit with a thank-you
message and a copy of answers by email when the spec has it.

Quiz: scoring per option, a results page with the score, pass or fail, and explanations;
share your result. Poll: live results bars after voting, one vote per device.

Builder (/admin): add and reorder questions, settings (open or closed, limit, dates,
theme), share link and QR, responses with filters, charts per question, export.

Data: forms (title, description, settings, theme), questions (type, label, options,
required, logic), responses and answers, quiz scores.

Minimums for a first build: the form of the spec fully working with validation and the
thank-you page, 20 seeded responses, the builder editing questions, the responses view
with charts and export.
