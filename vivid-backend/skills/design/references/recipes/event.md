# Recipe: event (a conference, concert, festival, wedding or workshop with tickets)

Pages: Home (the event), Schedule or Lineup, Speakers or Artists, Tickets, Venue, FAQ. For
an organiser running several events: Events list and one page per event. Owner area at
/admin: ticket sales, check-in list, attendees export.

Home: full-bleed hero with the event name as the biggest type on the page, date and place
in an eyebrow, a countdown, one Get tickets button; a "why come" band with three reasons;
the lineup or speakers as a grid of portraits (kind=photo, Black African people); the
schedule preview; ticket tiers as cards (name, price, what is included, sold-out state);
venue with a map or address card and directions; sponsors strip; FAQ accordion; footer with
organiser contact and socials.

Tickets: tiers with quantity steppers, an order summary, buyer details, Paystack payment,
a confirmation page with a QR code per ticket (a code the check-in list can look up) and
"add to calendar".

Schedule: day tabs, time-ordered sessions with room and speaker; Speakers: cards with bio
sheets. Venue: address, how to get there, parking, accessibility.

Data: event (name, dates, venue, description), tiers (name, price, quantity, sold),
sessions, speakers, orders and tickets (code, tier, buyer, checked_in).

Minimums for a first build: hero with countdown, at least 8 speakers or artists, a full
day of schedule, 3 tiers, ticket purchase to the QR confirmation, admin at /admin with
sales and a searchable check-in list.
