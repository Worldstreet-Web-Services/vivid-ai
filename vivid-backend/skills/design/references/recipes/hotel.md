# Recipe: hotel (a hotel, guesthouse, short-let or resort with room booking)

Pages: Home, Rooms, Room, Book (availability and checkout), Amenities or Experiences,
Gallery, Contact and Location. Owner area at /admin: bookings calendar, rooms and rates,
guests.

Home: a cinematic hero photo (the pool, the lobby, a room at dusk), an availability bar
(check-in, check-out, guests) that leads to Rooms; room types preview with prices from;
amenities icons row; a gallery strip; reviews; location with directions; footer with
phone, WhatsApp, address.

Rooms: cards with a photo carousel, name, sleeps, bed type, size, amenities pills, price
per night, Book; filters by guests and price. Room: gallery, description, amenities,
rates by date, policies, the booking panel sticky on desktop.

Book: dates and guests, room and rate, extras (breakfast, airport pickup), guest details,
Paystack or pay at the hotel, a confirmation with the booking reference and check-in
details; the availability calendar blocks booked nights.

Data: rooms (name, type, sleeps, beds, size, amenities, photos, base_rate, quantity),
rates by date, bookings (room, check_in, check_out, guests, extras, total, status),
guests, reviews.

Minimums for a first build: 6 room types with 3 photos each, a working availability check
with a calendar, booking to the confirmation, admin at /admin with the bookings calendar
and room editor.
