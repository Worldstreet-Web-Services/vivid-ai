# Recipe: rental (cars, equipment, dresses, cameras, spaces: book by day or hour)

Pages: Home, Browse, Item, Book, My rentals, List your item (owner), Contact. Owner area at
/admin: bookings calendar, items, deposits.

Home: hero with the hero item photo, a search (what, dates), categories, featured items
with price per day, how it works (choose, book, pick up or delivery, return), trust
(deposit, insurance, ID check), reviews, footer.

Browse: filters (category, price, dates available, location), cards with photo, name,
price per day or hour, rating, Book. Item: gallery, specs, rules, availability calendar,
price breakdown (days, delivery, deposit), Book panel.

Book: dates or hours, delivery or pickup, ID and phone, deposit and total, Paystack or
pay at pickup, confirmation with pickup instructions; returns and deposit refund tracked
by admin.

Data: items (name, category, description, photos, price_per_day, deposit, quantity,
location, rules), bookings (item, from, to, delivery, total, deposit, status), customers,
reviews.

Minimums for a first build: 12 items across 4 categories with photos, an availability
calendar that blocks booked dates, booking to confirmation, admin at /admin with the
calendar and items.
