# Recipe: fitness (a gym, studio, trainer or wellness centre with classes and memberships)

Pages: Home, Classes (timetable), Memberships, Trainers, Book, My account, Contact. Owner
area at /admin: bookings, members, timetable editor.

Home: high-energy hero (a Black African athlete mid-movement, dramatic light, or a dark
gradient with type), the promise, Join and Book a class; today's classes strip; membership
tiers preview; trainers grid; results or testimonials with before-and-after style cards;
location and hours; footer.

Classes: a weekly timetable (day tabs on phones, a grid on desktop) with class name,
time, trainer, spots left, Book; filters by type; class detail sheet.

Memberships: tiers as cards (monthly, quarterly, annual; what is included; the popular
one lifted), sign up to a member account with Paystack when the spec has it; My account
shows the plan, next payment, booked classes, attendance.

Book: pick a class and slot, confirm, cancellation rules; a member can cancel up to the
cut-off. Trainers: cards with specialities and a booking link for personal training.

Data: classes (name, type, trainer, capacity, duration), sessions (class, starts_at,
booked), bookings, memberships and plans, trainers, members.

Minimums for a first build: a full week of sessions across 8 class types, 5 trainers with
portraits, 3 membership tiers, booking to confirmation with spots decreasing, admin at
/admin with bookings and the timetable editor.
