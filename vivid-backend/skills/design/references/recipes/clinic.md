# Recipe: clinic (a hospital, clinic, dentist, lab or telehealth with appointments)

Pages: Home, Services, Doctors, Book an appointment, Patient portal (signed in), About,
Contact and Emergency. Owner area at /admin: appointments by day, doctors and schedules,
patients.

Home: calm hero (a Black African doctor with a patient, soft light), the reassurance
line, Book and Call; services as cards with icons; doctors grid with specialities;
insurance and payment strip; how to find us with hours and an emergency number always
visible; testimonials; footer.

Book: service, doctor (or first available), date and slot from the doctor's schedule,
patient details, reason, confirmation with instructions and a reminder note; reschedule
and cancel from the portal. Portal: upcoming and past appointments, results or documents
when the spec has them, profile.

Doctors: cards with photo, speciality, languages, days, Book. Services: what, how long,
price where the spec gives it.

Data: services, doctors (name, speciality, bio, photo, schedule), appointments (doctor,
patient, service, starts_at, status, notes), patients (profiles), documents.

Minimums for a first build: 8 services, 6 doctors with portraits and schedules, booking to
confirmation with real slot availability, the patient portal with appointments, admin at
/admin with the day view and doctor schedules.
