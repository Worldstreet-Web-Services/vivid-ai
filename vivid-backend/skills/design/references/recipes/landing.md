# Recipe: landing page (bakery, restaurant, agency, product)

One page, six to eight sections, each earning its place:

1 Header: logo, three to five anchor links, one action (Order, Call, Get a quote).
2 Hero: the promise in one line, one supporting sentence, primary action, the best photo
  large (aspect-[4/3] on phones, half width on desktop).
3 Proof or menu: the products or services as a 2/3/4 grid with photo, name, price or one
  line each.
4 How it works or why us: three points with an icon each, one sentence each.
5 A full-width photo or quote band to break the rhythm.
6 Hours, location and contact: a two-column block (details and a map image or address
  card), WhatsApp and call buttons.
7 Footer.

On phones every section stacks, headings shrink one step, the primary action repeats in
the footer. No carousel; stack the images.
