# Recipe: community (a forum, group or social feed for a club, church, alumni or interest)

Pages: Home (feed), Topics or Groups, Post, Members, Events, Profile, Sign in and Sign up.
Owner area at /admin: reported posts, members, roles.

Home: a feed of posts (author avatar and name, time, text, optional photo, likes,
comments count) with a composer at the top for signed-in members; a right rail on desktop
with trending topics, upcoming events and new members; a welcome card for visitors that
explains the community and asks them to join.

Post: the post, comments threaded one level, a reply box, like and report actions.
Topics: tiles with post counts; Members: a grid with search; Profile: avatar, bio, posts.

Rules: real names or handles, moderation queue for reports, a member can edit or delete
their own content, an admin can pin and remove; notifications list for replies and likes.

Data: profiles (name, handle, avatar, bio, role), posts (author, text, photo, topic,
pinned), comments, likes, reports, events, memberships.

Minimums for a first build: sign up and sign in, 20 seeded posts across 6 topics with
comments and likes, working composer and comments, 12 member profiles with generated
portraits (Black African), admin at /admin with reports and members.
