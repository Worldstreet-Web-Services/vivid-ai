# Recipe: directory (a listings site: businesses, professionals, schools, churches, vendors)

Pages: Home (search), Listings, Listing, Category, Add your listing, Claim or Sign in.
Owner area at /admin: approvals, categories, featured.

Home: hero with a search (what, where) over a city photo or a shape composition; popular
categories as icon tiles; featured listings; "recently added"; how to get listed; stats;
footer.

Listings: filters (category, area, rating, open now), result cards with logo or photo,
name, category, area, rating, a verified badge, Call and WhatsApp; map when a maps key
exists. Listing: gallery, about, services or menu, hours with open-now, address and
directions, contact buttons, reviews with a form, "suggest an edit", similar listings.

Add your listing: a stepped form (business, category, contact, hours, photos) saved as
pending; approved from /admin; the owner can claim and edit with a sign-in.

Data: listings (name, slug, category, description, address, area, phone, whatsapp,
website, hours, photos, verified, status, owner), categories, reviews, claims.

Minimums for a first build: 24 listings across 8 categories and 4 areas with a photo or
generated mark each, working search and filters, listing page with reviews saving, the
add-listing flow to pending, admin at /admin with approvals.
