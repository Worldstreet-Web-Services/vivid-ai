# Recipe: membership (a club, association, alumni body or paid community with dues)

Pages: Home, About, Benefits, Join, Events, Members directory (signed in), Dues and
payments, Account. Owner area at /admin: members, dues, events, announcements.

Home: hero with the association's identity (crest or generated mark, group photo),
Join and Sign in; benefits as cards; upcoming events; announcements; leadership with
portraits; footer.

Join: tiers (student, regular, life), a form (details, category, referee), payment of
dues (Paystack) or bank transfer proof upload, pending until approved, then a member
card page (name, number, tier, valid until, QR).

Members directory: search by name, chapter, profession; profile cards with contact
preferences; Dues: history, next due, Pay now; Events: RSVP and attendance.

Data: members (profile, number, tier, chapter, status, joined_at), dues (member, period,
amount, paid_at, reference), events and RSVPs, announcements, leadership.

Minimums for a first build: join flow to pending and then approved, 30 seeded members
across 3 chapters, dues with paid and unpaid states, 4 events, admin at /admin with
members, dues and announcements.
