# Recipe: real-estate (property listings for sale or rent, an agency or a landlord)

Pages: Home, Listings (search and filters), Property, Agents or About, Sell or Let with us,
Contact. Owner area at /admin: listings editor, enquiries.

Home: hero with a search bar (location, buy or rent, price range, bedrooms) over a
lifestyle photo of a Lagos-style property; featured listings carousel; "browse by area"
tiles; how it works for buyers and for landlords; agent introductions; testimonials;
footer with office address and phone.

Listings: filters in a sidebar (desktop) or a sheet (phone): type, price, bedrooms,
bathrooms, area, furnished; results as cards with a photo, price large, address line,
bed and bath and size icons, a "new" or "reduced" badge; sort; map toggle when a maps
key exists.

Property: gallery (5 or more photos, a lightbox), price and key facts strip, description,
features list, floor and size, location card, agent card with Call and WhatsApp, an
enquiry form, similar properties.

Data: listings (title, type, status, price, address, area, beds, baths, size, features,
photos, agent), agents, enquiries, saved listings (browser).

Minimums for a first build: 12 listings across at least 3 areas with 3 or more photos
each, working filters and sort, a property page with the enquiry form saving, admin at
/admin with the listings editor and enquiries.
