# Recipe: crowdfunding (raise money for a cause, a project, a person or a product)

Pages: Home, Campaigns, Campaign, Donate or Back, Start a campaign, Account (my
campaigns and donations). Owner area at /admin: approvals, payouts, reports.

Home: hero with a campaign story (portrait, generated, Black African), the platform line,
Start a campaign and Browse; featured campaigns as cards with a progress bar, raised of
goal, days left, supporters; categories; how it works; trust and fees; success stories;
footer.

Campaign: cover, title, organiser, progress bar with raised, goal, supporters, days left,
Donate panel sticky (presets, custom, anonymous toggle, message), the story, updates,
donors wall, share buttons, report link. Donate: amount, details, Paystack, thank-you with
share.

Start a campaign: stepped form (goal, category, story, cover, bank details for payout),
pending until approved; organiser dashboard with donations and post an update.

Data: campaigns (title, slug, goal, raised, category, story, cover, organiser, status,
ends_at), donations (campaign, amount, donor, anonymous, message, reference), updates,
payouts.

Minimums for a first build: 10 campaigns with covers across 5 categories and realistic
progress, donation to thank-you, start-a-campaign to pending, organiser dashboard, admin
at /admin with approvals and payouts.
