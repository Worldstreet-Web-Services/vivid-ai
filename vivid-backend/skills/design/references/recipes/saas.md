# Recipe: saas (a software product with a marketing site and a logged-in app)

Two faces: the marketing site (platform recipe rules apply: light, one deep primary, one
bright partner, product mockups) and the app itself behind sign-in.

Marketing pages: Home, Features, Pricing, Customers or Use cases, Docs or Help, Blog
(optional), Sign in, Sign up. App pages: Dashboard, the core object list and detail
(projects, invoices, campaigns, whatever the spec's noun is), Team, Billing, Settings.
Owner area at /admin: accounts, plans, usage.

Home: eyebrow pill, three-line headline, one paragraph, Start free and Book a demo, a
ProductMockup of the dashboard with seeded data, logos strip, three benefit tiles, feature
rows with mockups, pricing preview, testimonials, dark CTA band, footer.

Pricing: three plans as cards with a monthly or yearly toggle, the popular one lifted,
features checklist, FAQ; Sign up leads to the app with the chosen plan.

App: a sidebar with the product's sections, a top bar with search and the account menu,
the dashboard with 4 stat cards and a chart, tables with filters and row actions, forms in
sheets, empty states that teach, a Team page with invites and roles, Billing with the plan
and invoices (Paystack when the spec has it).

Data: accounts (workspaces), memberships with roles, the core objects, plans,
subscriptions, invoices, activity.

Minimums for a first build: the full marketing site, sign up to a dashboard with seeded
data, the core object's list, create and detail working, Team and Billing pages, admin at
/admin.
